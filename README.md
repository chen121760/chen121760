### ✎ About me

I am a M.S. candidate in **Materials Physics and Chemistry** at **Guangdong University of Technology**, working on crystal structure prediction, machine learning, and first-principles calculations. I expect to graduate in June 2027 and am looking for a **PhD position starting Fall 2027**.

### ✦ Selected projects

#### [USPEX-Analyzer](https://github.com/chen121760/USPEX-Analyzer)
Browser-based analysis platform for **USPEX** results: convex hull, Pareto front, genealogy tracking. · [live demo](https://chen121760.github.io/USPEX-Analyzer/)

#### [SISSO-Analyzer](https://github.com/chen121760/SISSO-Analyzer)
Interactive analyzer for **SISSO** regression results: validate, compare and visualize candidate models. · [live demo](https://chen121760.github.io/SISSO-Analyzer/)

#### [SISSO-Sage](https://github.com/chen121760/SISSO-Sage)
AI-assisted evidence review and candidate selection for **SISSO** models.

#### [Hydride-Tc-Predictor](https://github.com/chen121760/Hydride-Tc-Predictor)
Interpretable symbolic-regression predictor for **Tc** of hydrogen-based superconductors. · [online calculator](https://chen121760.github.io/web-of-Hydride-Tc-Predictor/)
